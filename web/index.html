Features

One arena, many ways to practise

Duel

Chart slices with the date and real price hidden. Buy, sell or skip before time runs out. Challenge a friend with a code or practise solo.

Chart & replay

M1 to D1 charts, indicators, drawing tools and bar replay with paper trading.

Backtest & journal

Test automated strategies on 5 years of data and keep trades in a journal with screenshots.

Leaderboard

Total R from the last 7 days of duels. Your opponent’s result opens only once the duel is over.
